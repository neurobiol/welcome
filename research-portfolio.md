# Research Portfolio

This page provides a public overview of my current and recent research activities across quantum biology, neurobiology, memory and plasticity, artificial life, transcriptomics, quantum sensing, and AI/cognition.

My thesis work is focused on quantum-like adaptive-plasticity models for context-sensitive associative memory. The items below are related research, training, collaboration, dissemination, and service activities. They are listed here as a living research portfolio rather than as thesis deliverables.

---

## Current thesis focus

**Quantum-like adaptive plasticity for context-sensitive associative memory**

This work develops and tests adaptive-plasticity models of memory using matched quantum-like, real-valued no-phase, and Markov-rate model families. The broader goal is to understand how recall strength, order sensitivity, context dependence, interference/recovery, and stage structure can be preserved across memory dynamics.
